# Voice & Participation

The defining constraint of this track: the user participates actively — their words carry the
post. Agents support; they don't ghostwrite.

## Areas under consideration
- [ ] The participation model — which parts the user always writes vs where agents help
- [ ] What agents may do — outline, restructure, tighten, suggest; the allowed verbs
- [ ] What agents may not do — the line that keeps the voice authentic
- [ ] Voice markers — the traits of your writing to preserve during any edit
- [ ] Editing etiquette — propose changes vs apply them; how suggestions are surfaced
