# Section Writing

The craft of each section, drafted against the contracts set in
`papers/2-design/structure-and-argument`.

## Areas under consideration
- [ ] Abstract — the formula (problem, gap, approach, result, implication) and length discipline
- [ ] Introduction — hooking, contribution bullets, how much related work belongs here
- [ ] Methods — reproducibility bar, detail level, what goes to appendix
- [ ] Results — findings vs interpretation separation, ordering
- [ ] Discussion/limitations — honest framing without undermining the work
- [ ] Related work — organizing by theme vs chronology, fair characterization
- [ ] Titles & headings — informative over clever?
