# Revision & Review

The verify phase for papers — a draft is not done when it's written.

## Areas under consideration
- [ ] Pass structure — the ordered revision passes (argument → clarity → humanize → mechanics?)
- [ ] Clarity editing — sentence-level rules (one idea per sentence, cut ruthlessly)
- [ ] Consistency checks — terminology, notation, tense, figure/table numbering
- [ ] Citation verification — every claim cited correctly, no hallucinated references
- [ ] Adversarial read — reviewing as a hostile reviewer would; the checklist
- [ ] External feedback — when to get human readers and what to ask them
