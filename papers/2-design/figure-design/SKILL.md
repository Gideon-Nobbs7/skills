# Figure Design

What each figure should be, before it's made. Production mechanics live in
`papers/3-build/figure-production`.

## Areas under consideration
- [ ] One claim per figure — matching chart type to the claim being made
- [ ] Clarity rules — labeling, legends, annotation directly on the figure
- [ ] Style consistency — fonts, colors, sizing uniform across a paper's figures
- [ ] Figure 1 — the overview/teaser figure and what it must communicate
- [ ] Honesty rules — axes, baselines, error bars; never misleading
- [ ] Grayscale/print survivability and colorblind safety
